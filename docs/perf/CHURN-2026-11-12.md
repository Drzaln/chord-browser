# WebContent process churn, first re-count (c1k.18, 2026-11-12)

**Not the controlled run the bead asks for.** This is read from the system
log of the installed 1.26.0 during ordinary use (11:38 to 15:58, 4.3 hours,
one relaunch at 15:57), with the user's own tabs and extensions. No tab set,
no front/hidden/minimized phases, and nothing was changed to get it.

    /usr/bin/log show --last 6h --predicate 'process == "Chord" AND
      (eventMessage CONTAINS "WebProcessProxy::didFinishLaunching" OR
       eventMessage CONTAINS "WebProcessProxy::shutDown" OR
       subsystem == "com.rizal.chord")'

## What it shows

| | 2026-10-30 baseline | this count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WebContent launches | 12 per 5 minutes, in every phase | 32 in 4.3 hours (about 0.6 per 5 minutes) |
| Busiest 5 minutes | — | 10 (13:56 to 13:58, while an extension was being loaded) |
| Lifetime of those that exited (28) | mean ~27 s | median ~33 s; 19 lived under a minute |

- **Bitwarden no longer churns.** Its worker hold is renewed every ~10 minutes
  (32 "holding worker" lines, 605 to 617 s apart) and only one launch falls
  near any of them. That is c1k.9 and c1k.14 holding.
- **Launches come in bursts, not on a timer.** 19 of the 32 are between 13:40
  and 13:58; hours pass with one or none.
- **Launches by what was logged within 3 s of them:** 17 nothing, 5 app launch
  (four processes that each lived 30 to 33 s), 5 another extension's worker
  being held (`ddkjiahej…`, three processes of 9 to 13 s), 3 the window being
  shown, 1 a tab switch, 1 a Bitwarden hold.

## What it does not show

- The 17 unexplained launches. Chord logged nothing near them, so they cannot
  be told apart: a new tab, a navigation that swapped process, a service
  worker, a prewarmed process. Many live almost exactly 30, 33 or 41 s, which
  looks like a process WebKit starts and drops when it is not used, but that
  is a guess.
- Whether the steady state meets the bead's bar (at most 4 per 5 minutes after
  the first 2, in front, hidden and minimized). Ordinary use is not steady
  state.

## Changed so the next count can say more

`perf web-view <kind>` is now logged wherever Chord itself asks for a web
view: `tab`, `popup`, `extension-page` (WebKitEngine) and `extension-hidden`
(the extension data-clearing page). A launch with none of these near it is
then WebKit's own doing.
